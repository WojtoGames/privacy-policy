Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 7, 2026

This privacy policy explains what data is collected while using the mobile game Grain ("the App"), and how that data is used.

1. Data stored only on your device

The App saves your game progress (including coin count, high score, chosen colors and backgrounds, and daily reward status) only locally, in your phone's storage. This data is never sent to any server, we have no access to it, and it is never linked to you as a person in any way. Uninstalling the App permanently deletes this data.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The App displays ads provided by Google AdMob (a service owned by Google LLC). To make this work, AdMob may collect and process:

  • the device's Advertising ID
  • approximate location (based on IP address)
  • technical information about the device and operating system
  • ad interaction data (e.g., whether an ad was watched to completion)

This data is collected and processed by Google in accordance with their own privacy policy, not by us directly. More information:

Google Privacy Policy
How AdMob uses data

3. No account or personal data

The App does not require creating an account or providing an email address, name, phone number, or any other personal information. We do not collect such information anywhere in the App.

4. Children

The App is not knowingly directed at children under the age of 13 and does not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided us with personal data, please contact us at the address below.

5. App permissions

The App may require internet access (to display ads) and, optionally, access to device vibration (haptic feedback in the game, which can be turned off in settings). It does not request access to the camera, microphone, contacts, GPS location, or files.

6. Changes to this privacy policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time, for example due to changes in the App or legal requirements. We will indicate any significant changes by updating the date at the top of this page.
